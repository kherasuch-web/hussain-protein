This policy explains what the Hussain Protein app (Android and Windows) keeps, why, and who can see it. The app is made by Hussain Protein Group for its own broiler farms.

1. What we keep

  • Login: username, password (kept safely by Google Firebase, we cannot read it) and, if the person adds it, their Gmail address for "Forgot password".
  • Farm record: what the farm staff write: daily feed, mortality, medicine, water, weight, sale, spendings, salary and dealer names.
  • Phone name: the model name of each phone that logs in (for example "Samsung A15"), so the owner can see and remove phones.

We do not collect location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal data.

2. Where it is kept

The record is kept in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication), protected by login rules, so each farm sees only its own farm and the owner sees all farms. PDF reports are saved only on your own phone or computer.

3. Google Drive backup

If the owner connects a Google account, the app saves a daily backup file into that account's own Google Drive, in the folder "Hussain Protein Backup". The app uses only the drive.file permission: it can see only the files it made itself, nothing else in the Drive. Passwords are never in the backup.

4. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with anyone. There are no ads and no tracking. Data leaves the app only when a user shares a PDF or message themselves (for example on WhatsApp).

5. Delete your data

The owner can remove any login or phone in the app. To delete a login or a farm's full record, email us and it will be deleted within 30 days.

6. Children

The app is for farm work and is not meant for children under 13.
