What is this app?

Hussain Protein is a private app for our own broiler farms. The farm staff write the daily farm record in it, and the owner sees every farm on the phone or computer.

What it does

Daily report
Feed, mortality, medicine and water every day, weekly FCR.
Feed and medicine
Feed in store, feed eaten, medicine bought from each dealer.
Sale and spendings
Bird sale bills, expenses and labour salary.
Reports
PDF reports and bills, shared on WhatsApp.
Supervisor
The supervisor sends his daily data to the farm.
Backup
A daily backup goes to the owner's own Google Drive.

Who can use it

Only people who are given a username and password by the farm owner can log in. There is no public sign up.
